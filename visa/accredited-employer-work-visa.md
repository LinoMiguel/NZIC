## What is the AEWV?

The Accredited Employer Work Visa (AEWV) is New Zealand's primary employer-sponsored work visa. It replaced several previous work visa categories from July 2022.

To get an AEWV, three things must be in place:

1. The employer must be **accredited** with Immigration New Zealand
2. The employer must have completed a **Job Check** — confirming the role, pay, and that no suitable NZ worker could fill it
3. The worker can then apply for the **AEWV** using the Job Check approval reference

## Employer accreditation

Accreditation confirms that an employer meets INZ's standards for employing migrant workers. There are two levels:
- **Standard accreditation** (up to 5 migrant workers)
- **High-volume accreditation** (6+ migrant workers)

Accreditation must be renewed periodically. NZIC helps employers with the accreditation process — see [Employer Services](/job-search/accredited-employers).

## Pay requirements

The AEWV requires the employer to pay at least the **New Zealand median wage** for most roles. Some sectors and occupations have higher minimum pay thresholds. Your adviser will confirm the current requirement for your situation.

## Pathway to residence

After working in New Zealand on an AEWV for a qualifying period, workers in eligible occupations may be able to apply for residence under the [Green List](/visa/the-green-list) or the [Skilled Migrant Category](/visa/skilled-migrant-category).

If you have a job offer from a New Zealand employer, the first step is confirming whether that employer is accredited (or is willing to become accredited). A [free assessment](/free-assessment) will help you understand your options.
