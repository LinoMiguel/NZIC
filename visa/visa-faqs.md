  <div class="faq-list">

    <h2 style="font-family:var(--font-heading);font-size:1.3rem;font-weight:700;color:var(--navy);margin:2rem 0 1rem;">Visas</h2>

    <div class="faq-item">
      <button class="faq-question" aria-expanded="false">What is the difference between a work visa and residence? <span class="faq-chevron" aria-hidden="true">▾</span></button>
      <div class="faq-answer"><div class="faq-answer-inner">A work visa is temporary — it allows you to live and work in New Zealand for a fixed period. Residence (a "permanent resident visa" or "resident visa") is indefinite and gives you most of the rights of a citizen, including the right to live, work, and study in New Zealand without time limits. Most migrants start on a work visa and transition to residence.</div></div>
    </div>

    <div class="faq-item">
      <button class="faq-question" aria-expanded="false">Can I bring my family with me? <span class="faq-chevron" aria-hidden="true">▾</span></button>
      <div class="faq-answer"><div class="faq-answer-inner">Yes, in most cases. Partners and dependent children can usually be included on the same visa application or apply for a secondary visa. The specific options depend on the visa category. Your adviser will confirm what applies to your situation.</div></div>
    </div>

    <div class="faq-item">
      <button class="faq-question" aria-expanded="false">I do not have a job offer. Can I still apply for residence? <span class="faq-chevron" aria-hidden="true">▾</span></button>
      <div class="faq-answer"><div class="faq-answer-inner">It is very difficult to reach the Skilled Migrant Category points threshold without a job offer. Some Green List occupations can go straight to residence with qualifications and experience but without a prior job offer. The [Free Assessment](/free-assessment) is the best starting point — a licensed adviser will tell you honestly what your options are.</div></div>
    </div>

    <div class="faq-item">
      <button class="faq-question" aria-expanded="false">How long does the SMC process take? <span class="faq-chevron" aria-hidden="true">▾</span></button>
      <div class="faq-answer"><div class="faq-answer-inner">From Expression of Interest through to a residence visa decision, the process typically takes 4–9 months for straightforward cases. Complex cases or those requiring additional evidence take longer. See the <a href="/visa/processing-times">Processing Times</a> page for current estimates.</div></div>
    </div>

    <div class="faq-item">
      <button class="faq-question" aria-expanded="false">What is an NZQA assessment and do I need one? <span class="faq-chevron" aria-hidden="true">▾</span></button>
      <div class="faq-answer"><div class="faq-answer-inner">An NZQA (New Zealand Qualifications Authority) assessment compares your overseas qualification against the New Zealand qualification framework. INZ requires this for most visa categories that recognise qualifications. The process takes several weeks and must usually be completed before lodging a visa application. Your adviser will tell you whether and when to initiate this.</div></div>
    </div>

    <div class="faq-item">
      <button class="faq-question" aria-expanded="false">What English requirements apply? <span class="faq-chevron" aria-hidden="true">▾</span></button>
      <div class="faq-answer"><div class="faq-answer-inner">Most work and residence visa categories require evidence of English ability, typically through IELTS, TOEFL, or similar. The specific score required depends on the visa category and occupation. Citizens of English-speaking countries (UK, USA, Australia, Canada, Ireland, South Africa) are generally exempt. Your adviser will confirm whether a test is required in your case.</div></div>
    </div>

    <h2 style="font-family:var(--font-heading);font-size:1.3rem;font-weight:700;color:var(--navy);margin:2rem 0 1rem;">Pricing</h2>

    <div class="faq-item">
      <button class="faq-question" aria-expanded="false">How much does NZIC charge? <span class="faq-chevron" aria-hidden="true">▾</span></button>
      <div class="faq-answer"><div class="faq-answer-inner">Fees depend on the complexity of your situation and the services required. After reviewing your free assessment, your adviser will provide a clear written quote. The initial free assessment has no cost. INZ also charges its own application fees — your adviser will advise on these as part of your cost estimate.</div></div>
    </div>

    <div class="faq-item">
      <button class="faq-question" aria-expanded="false">Is the initial assessment really free? <span class="faq-chevron" aria-hidden="true">▾</span></button>
      <div class="faq-answer"><div class="faq-answer-inner">Yes, completely. Submit your assessment details and a Licensed Immigration Adviser will review your situation and respond personally — at no cost and with no obligation to proceed.</div></div>
    </div>

    <div class="faq-item">
      <button class="faq-question" aria-expanded="false">What happens if my application is declined? <span class="faq-chevron" aria-hidden="true">▾</span></button>
      <div class="faq-answer"><div class="faq-answer-inner">Declines are uncommon for well-prepared applications. If INZ declines an application NZIC prepared, your adviser will review the decision, advise on whether a reconsideration or appeal is appropriate, and explore alternative pathways. NZIC's focus on thorough preparation is specifically to avoid this outcome.</div></div>
    </div>
