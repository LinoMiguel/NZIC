## The 8-step visa process

Working with NZIC follows a clear, predictable path:

1. **Immigration strategy** — your adviser reviews your situation, identifies the best pathway, and sets realistic expectations on timeline and cost.
2. **Documents and guidelines** — you receive a personalised checklist of every document INZ will require, with guidance on what counts and what format.
3. **International Qualification Assessment** — if your qualifications were earned overseas, your adviser guides you through NZQA assessment and any employer recognition steps.
4. **The Catch-22** — if your pathway requires a job offer, your adviser explains the landscape honestly, including what a Fusion Service engagement would look like.
5. **Solving the Catch-22** — job search strategy, hidden market access, and direct introductions where appropriate. See the [Fusion Service](/about/fusion-service) for details.
6. **Submission** — your adviser reviews every document, prepares the application, and submits it to INZ on your behalf.
7. **Processing** — your adviser monitors the application, responds to any INZ requests for further information, and keeps you updated.
8. **Visa outcome** — on approval, your adviser explains your visa conditions, rights, and next steps. On decline (rare), they advise on appeal or alternative pathways.
