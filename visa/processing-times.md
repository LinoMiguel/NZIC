## Processing time estimates

| Visa type | Indicative processing time | Notes |
|-----------|---------------------------|-------|
| Skilled Migrant Category (residence) | 4–9 months | From invitation to apply; varies by complexity |
| Accredited Employer Work Visa | 4–10 weeks | After Job Check approval; straightforward cases |
| Partner of NZ Citizen/Resident (work) | 4–8 weeks | May be faster for straightforward cases |
| Partner of NZ Citizen/Resident (residence) | 3–9 months | Depends on relationship evidence |
| Visitor Visa | 1–4 weeks | Online applications are faster |
| Student Visa | 4–8 weeks | Depends on institution and study level |
| Active Investor Plus | 4–8 months | Complex investment verification required |

**Figures last checked:** November 2026. Source: [Immigration New Zealand ↗](https://www.immigration.govt.nz)

## Factors that affect processing time

- **Completeness of documentation:** incomplete applications are put on hold and time pauses until the missing information is received
- **Health and character checks:** police clearances and medical certificates add time if issues arise
- **Case complexity:** straightforward applications with clear documentation process faster
- **INZ workload:** processing times fluctuate seasonally and in response to policy changes
