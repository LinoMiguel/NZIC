## What is the Green List?

The Green List is a list of occupations New Zealand has identified as being in high demand. Workers in these occupations may qualify for residence on a faster track than the standard Skilled Migrant Category.

## Two tiers

### Tier 1 — Straight to residence
Workers in Tier 1 occupations can apply for residence directly, without first needing a work visa or the SMC points process, provided they meet the specific criteria for their occupation.

Common Tier 1 occupations include many medical specialists, some engineering roles, and senior trade supervisors. Check the [official INZ list](https://www.immigration.govt.nz) for the current Tier 1 occupations.

INZ projects **80% of Tier 1 (Straight to Residence) applications processed within 5 months**.

### Tier 2 — Work to residence
Workers in Tier 2 occupations can get an Accredited Employer Work Visa and, after two years of employment in that role, apply for residence under the Green List pathway.

## Qualification and registration requirements

Most Green List occupations require:
- A recognised qualification at the relevant level
- Professional registration or licensing in New Zealand (e.g. Medical Council registration, Engineering NZ membership)
- Two years of post-qualification experience in the occupation

Requirements vary by occupation — your adviser will confirm exactly what applies to you.
