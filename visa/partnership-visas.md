## Partnership visas explained

New Zealand has two main partnership visa pathways:

1. **Partnership Work Visa** — if your partner holds a valid New Zealand work visa, you may be eligible for a work visa based on that relationship
2. **Partner of a New Zealand Citizen or Resident** — this pathway leads to residence for genuine, stable partnerships with NZ citizens or residents

## Eligibility

To qualify for the Partner of a New Zealand Citizen or Resident pathway, you must show:

- A genuine and stable relationship (married or de facto)
- Your partner is a New Zealand citizen or holds a current residence visa
- You meet INZ's health and character requirements
- In the case of a de facto relationship of less than 12 months: a Partner Temporary Visa may be granted first

## What "genuine and stable" means

INZ will assess the genuineness and stability of your relationship based on evidence such as:
- Length of the relationship
- Whether you have lived together
- Evidence of shared finances, property, or children
- Communication records if you have been apart

Your adviser will help you build a comprehensive evidence file that tells your story clearly and completely.

## Processing

Partnership residence applications are assessed on a case-by-case basis. Processing times vary. See the [Processing Times](/visa/processing-times) page for current estimates.
