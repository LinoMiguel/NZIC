## Residence pathways at a glance

New Zealand offers several routes to permanent residence. The right one depends on your skills, employment, family connections, and financial situation.

| Pathway | Who it suits | Key requirement |
|---------|-------------|-----------------|
| Skilled Migrant Category | Qualified workers with job offers | Points threshold + skilled employment |
| Green List | Workers in high-demand occupations | Occupation on the list + qualifications |
| Accredited Employer Work Visa → Residence | Workers sponsored by an NZ employer | 2+ years on AEWV in a qualifying role |
| Partnership (residence) | Partners of NZ citizens or residents | Genuine and stable relationship |
| Parent Resident Visa | Parents of NZ citizens | Ballot; limited places |
| Active Investor Plus | Investors with NZD 5M+ (Growth) or NZD 15M+ (Balanced) | Investment in NZ |
| Business Investor | Business owners with NZD 3M+ | Business experience + investment |
| Temporary Retirement | Retirees 66+ with NZD 750k+ | Age, health, financial threshold |

## Skilled work pathways

The [Skilled Migrant Category](/visa/skilled-migrant-category) and the [Green List](/visa/the-green-list) are the two main routes for skilled workers. Both ultimately require a qualifying job in New Zealand — which is where NZIC's [Fusion Service](/about/fusion-service) is designed to help.

## Family pathways

[Partnership visas](/visa/partnership-visas) allow spouses or de facto partners of NZ citizens and residents to live and work in New Zealand, with a pathway to residence. Parent and other family residence categories are more restricted.

## Investor and retirement pathways

New Zealand actively seeks investors and retirees with significant capital. See the [Retirement in NZ](/retirement-in-new-zealand) and [Investor and Business Visas](/investment) pages for financial thresholds.

## Finding the right pathway

The best pathway depends on your specific circumstances. A [free assessment](/free-assessment) with a Licensed Immigration Adviser is the most reliable way to identify your options.
