## What is the Skilled Migrant Category?

The Skilled Migrant Category (SMC) is New Zealand's principal residence pathway for internationally trained workers. It uses a points system to assess whether an applicant has the skills, qualifications, and experience New Zealand needs.

## Three SMC pathways

INZ recognises three distinct SMC pathways:

1. **Points-based pathway** — requires a minimum of 6 points (see table below)
2. **Skilled Work Experience pathway** — 5 years of skilled work experience in total, with at least 2 years in New Zealand
3. **Trades and Technician pathway** — a recognised qualification plus 4 years of experience, with at least 18 months of that experience in New Zealand

Your NZIC adviser will help you identify which pathway best fits your profile.

## Key eligibility criteria

To be considered under the SMC:

- You must be **55 years of age or younger** at the time of application
- You must meet INZ's health and character requirements
- You must qualify under one of the three pathways above

## Points table (points-based pathway)

Points are scored against the median wage. As of the last-reviewed date, the thresholds are:

| Points | Hourly rate threshold | Basis |
|--------|----------------------|-------|
| 1 point | NZD $50.34/hour | 1.5× the median wage |
| 2 points | NZD $67.12/hour | 2× the median wage |
| 3 points | NZD $100.68/hour | 3× the median wage |

Additional points are available for:

| Factor | Points |
|--------|--------|
| Skilled employment in NZ (job offer or current employment) | 160 |
| Partner with skilled employment in NZ | 20 |
| Work experience in a skilled occupation (2–5 years) | 10 |
| Work experience in a skilled occupation (6–9 years) | 20 |
| Work experience in a skilled occupation (10+ years) | 30 |
| NZ work experience in a skilled occupation (1–2 years) | 10 |
| NZ work experience in a skilled occupation (2+ years) | 20 |
| Recognised qualification (bachelor's level) | 50 |
| Recognised qualification (postgraduate) | 70 |
| Recognised qualification in an area of absolute skill shortage | +10 |
| Age 20–39 | 30 |
| Age 40–44 | 20 |
| Age 45–49 | 10 |
| Close family in NZ | 10 |
| Partner's qualifications | 10–20 |

> **Note:** INZ sets a minimum points threshold (the "pass mark") which changes over time. Applications are assessed in Expression of Interest rounds. This table is a guide only — verify current figures at the official INZ source linked above.

## The age requirement

You must be **55 or younger** at the date of your residence application. This is a single, consistent criterion — applicants who are 56 or older on the application date are not eligible under the SMC.

## Job offer requirements

A skilled job offer in New Zealand is worth 160 points — the highest single factor. Without a job offer, it is very difficult to reach the pass mark. This is at the core of the [Catch-22](/job-search/the-catch-22) that NZIC's Fusion Service addresses.

A "skilled" job for SMC purposes is one that INZ rates at ANZSCO Skill Level 1, 2, or 3.
