## New Zealand visa categories

New Zealand visas fall into four broad groups:

### Work visas
Temporary visas that allow you to live and work in New Zealand for a fixed period.

- **Accredited Employer Work Visa (AEWV)** — the main employer-sponsored work visa
- **Working Holiday Visa** — for young people (18–30, or 35 for some nationalities) from eligible countries
- **Skilled Migrant work visa** — transitional visa during SMC processing
- See [our full visa service page](/visa/our-visa-service) for all options

### Residence visas
Permanent or indefinite visas giving the right to live, work, and study in New Zealand without time limits.

- Skilled Migrant Category
- Green List pathways
- Partner of a NZ Citizen or Resident
- Parent Resident
- Active Investor Plus
- Business Investor
- See [Pathways to Residence](/visa/pathways-to-residence) for a full overview

### Visitor visas
Temporary visas for tourism, visiting family, or short-term activities. No work rights.

### Student visas
Allow overseas students to study in New Zealand for the duration of their course. Work rights are usually included.
