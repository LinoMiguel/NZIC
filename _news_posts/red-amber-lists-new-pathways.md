*Opinion piece by Dr. Carsten Hallwass, Principal Licensed Immigration Adviser, NZIC.*

INZ's introduction of Red and Amber Lists represents a significant shift in how New Zealand signals occupation demand to prospective migrants. The Red List identifies occupations in critical shortage where INZ is actively streamlining pathways. The Amber List covers occupations where demand exists but is more nuanced.

Alongside these lists, two important new residence pathways have been introduced that complement the existing Skilled Migrant Category and Green List framework. We discuss both pathways in detail with clients during our [free weekly webinar](/our-immigration-and-job-hunt-webinar).
