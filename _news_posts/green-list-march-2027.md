Immigration New Zealand has announced a series of changes to the Green List that will take effect from March 2027. The changes include additions to Tier 1 (Straight to Residence) and Tier 2 (Work to Residence) occupation lists, as well as amendments to minimum experience and registration requirements for certain occupations.

If your occupation is currently on the Green List — or you believe it should be — a [free assessment](/free-assessment) is the fastest way to understand what the changes mean for your situation.

The full list of updated occupations is available on the [INZ website](https://www.immigration.govt.nz/new-zealand-visas/preparing-a-visa-application/working-in-nz/green-list).
