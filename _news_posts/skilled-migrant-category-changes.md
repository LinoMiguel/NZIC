Immigration New Zealand has announced changes to the Skilled Migrant Category (SMC) Resident Visa. The amendments include revised points thresholds, updated hourly wage criteria, and clarifications to the three SMC pathways:

1. **Points-based pathway** — updated wage rate thresholds for 1, 2, and 3 points
2. **Skilled Work Experience pathway** — clarified requirements for the 5-year/2-year NZ experience criterion
3. **Trades and Technician pathway** — updated list of qualifying trade qualifications

The income thresholds for the points-based pathway are now tied to multiples of the median wage:

- 1 point: NZD $50.34/hour (1.5× median)
- 2 points: NZD $67.12/hour (2× median)
- 3 points: NZD $100.68/hour (3× median)
