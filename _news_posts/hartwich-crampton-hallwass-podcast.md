Dr. Carsten Hallwass, Principal Licensed Immigration Adviser and founder of NZIC, joined Oliver Hartwich (CEO, New Zealand Initiative) and Eric Crampton (Chief Economist, New Zealand Initiative) for a wide-ranging discussion on New Zealand immigration policy.

Topics covered include:

- The economic case for skilled migration and what New Zealand is getting right and wrong
- The Catch-22 that NZIC's Fusion Service was designed to solve
- How immigration policy interacts with the housing market, healthcare, and the labour market
- What prospective migrants should know about the current policy environment
- The case for a more transparent and merit-based immigration system

The podcast is available on the New Zealand Initiative website and major podcast platforms.
