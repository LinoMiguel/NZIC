    <h2 style="font-family:var(--font-heading);font-size:1.4rem;font-weight:700;color:var(--navy);margin-bottom:1rem;">Webinar FAQ</h2>
    <div class="faq-list">
      <div class="faq-item">
        <button class="faq-question" aria-expanded="false">Can I migrate using an AI-generated application? <span class="faq-chevron" aria-hidden="true">▾</span></button>
        <div class="faq-answer"><div class="faq-answer-inner">INZ requires truthful, accurate information from the applicant. AI-generated applications that are not carefully reviewed for accuracy, relevance, and honesty can lead to serious problems, including declines and bans. We cover how to use AI tools responsibly as a research aid, not a replacement for professional advice.</div></div>
      </div>
      <div class="faq-item">
        <button class="faq-question" aria-expanded="false">Can I qualify if my occupation is not on the Green List? <span class="faq-chevron" aria-hidden="true">▾</span></button>
        <div class="faq-answer"><div class="faq-answer-inner">Yes — the Skilled Migrant Category covers a much wider range of occupations than the Green List. If you have a skilled job offer or can get one, many occupations can qualify.</div></div>
      </div>
      <div class="faq-item">
        <button class="faq-question" aria-expanded="false">What do I do if NZ employers are not responding to my applications? <span class="faq-chevron" aria-hidden="true">▾</span></button>
        <div class="faq-answer"><div class="faq-answer-inner">This is very common for overseas applicants relying on job boards. The webinar covers in detail why this happens and what a more effective approach looks like — including accessing the hidden job market.</div></div>
      </div>
      <div class="faq-item">
        <button class="faq-question" aria-expanded="false">Are NZ interviews difficult? <span class="faq-chevron" aria-hidden="true">▾</span></button>
        <div class="faq-answer"><div class="faq-answer-inner">NZ employers tend to favour direct, conversational interviews and assess cultural fit as much as technical competence. We cover what to expect and how to prepare in the webinar.</div></div>
      </div>
      <div class="faq-item">
        <button class="faq-question" aria-expanded="false">I have only 6 points on the SMC — is there any chance? <span class="faq-chevron" aria-hidden="true">▾</span></button>
        <div class="faq-answer"><div class="faq-answer-inner">If you do not currently have enough points, we look at what it would take to reach the threshold — most commonly a job offer — and whether that is realistic within a reasonable timeframe for your situation.</div></div>
      </div>
      <div class="faq-item">
        <button class="faq-question" aria-expanded="false">Do I need an English test? <span class="faq-chevron" aria-hidden="true">▾</span></button>
        <div class="faq-answer"><div class="faq-answer-inner">It depends on your nationality and the visa category. Citizens of the UK, USA, Australia, Canada, Ireland, and South Africa are generally exempt. For others, INZ requires IELTS or equivalent at a specified minimum score. We cover this in the webinar.</div></div>
      </div>
      <div class="faq-item">
        <button class="faq-question" aria-expanded="false">Is an NZQA assessment mandatory? <span class="faq-chevron" aria-hidden="true">▾</span></button>
        <div class="faq-answer"><div class="faq-answer-inner">For most visa categories that recognise qualifications, yes. The NZQA assessment compares your overseas qualification to the NZ framework. We cover when to start this process and how long it takes.</div></div>
      </div>
      <div class="faq-item">
        <button class="faq-question" aria-expanded="false">Do I need to visit New Zealand before applying? <span class="faq-chevron" aria-hidden="true">▾</span></button>
        <div class="faq-answer"><div class="faq-answer-inner">No, it is not mandatory. Many clients never visit before they arrive to settle. However, a job-hunting visit can dramatically increase your chances of getting a job offer. We discuss the pros, cons, and practical options.</div></div>
      </div>
      <div class="faq-item">
        <button class="faq-question" aria-expanded="false">Why do NZ employers prefer to hire migrants? <span class="faq-chevron" aria-hidden="true">▾</span></button>
        <div class="faq-answer"><div class="faq-answer-inner">In shortage occupations, NZ employers sometimes cannot find suitable local candidates and are genuinely open to migrants. However, the process of hiring a migrant is more complex than hiring locally, which is why NZIC's employer services exist — to remove that complexity.</div></div>
      </div>
