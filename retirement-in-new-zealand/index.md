    <div class="prose" style="margin-top:2rem;">
      <h2>What "settlement funds" means</h2>
      <p>Settlement funds must be transferable to New Zealand and available for use in NZ. They can be in the form of cash, superannuation or pension funds, property equity (if realised), or other liquid assets. Your adviser will confirm what formats INZ accepts for your specific visa category.</p>

      <h2>Tax and financial advice</h2>
      <p>Moving retirement funds or investment capital to New Zealand has tax implications in both NZ and your current country of residence. NZIC strongly recommends independent legal, tax, and financial advice before committing to any investment or property purchase in NZ. NZIC provides immigration advice only and is not a licensed financial adviser or real-estate agent.</p>
