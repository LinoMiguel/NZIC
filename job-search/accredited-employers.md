<section class="section">
  <div class="container">

    <div class="process-steps" style="margin-bottom:4rem;">
      <div class="process-step">
        <div class="step-number">1</div>
        <h3 class="step-title">Employer Accreditation</h3>
        <p class="step-desc">Your business must be accredited with Immigration New Zealand before it can sponsor a migrant worker. Standard accreditation covers up to 5 migrant employees; high-volume accreditation covers more. NZIC helps you prepare and submit your accreditation application.</p>
        <a class="btn btn-outline" href="{{ '/visa/accredited-employer-work-visa' | relative_url }}" style="margin-top:1.5rem;">AEWV explained →</a>
      </div>
      <div class="process-step">
        <div class="step-number">2</div>
        <h3 class="step-title">Job Check</h3>
        <p class="step-desc">Once accredited, you submit a Job Check for the specific role you want to fill. INZ confirms the job is genuine, the pay meets the median wage requirement, and that no suitable NZ worker could fill it. NZIC helps you build a compliant job check.</p>
      </div>
      <div class="process-step">
        <div class="step-number">3</div>
        <h3 class="step-title">Accredited Employer Work Visa</h3>
        <p class="step-desc">With a Job Check approval reference in hand, your chosen candidate can apply for an AEWV. NZIC can support the worker's visa application, ensuring it is complete and accurate for the fastest possible processing.</p>
      </div>
    </div>

    <div class="faq-list">
      <h2 style="font-family:var(--font-heading);font-size:1.4rem;font-weight:700;color:var(--navy);margin-bottom:1rem;">Employer FAQs</h2>
      <div class="faq-item">
        <button class="faq-question" aria-expanded="false">How long does accreditation take? <span class="faq-chevron" aria-hidden="true">▾</span></button>
        <div class="faq-answer"><div class="faq-answer-inner">INZ typically processes standard accreditation applications within 10 working days for straightforward cases. Applications with complex ownership structures or compliance history take longer. Starting the accreditation process before you have a specific candidate in mind is advisable.</div></div>
      </div>
      <div class="faq-item">
        <button class="faq-question" aria-expanded="false">What does the Job Check involve? <span class="faq-chevron" aria-hidden="true">▾</span></button>
        <div class="faq-answer"><div class="faq-answer-inner">A Job Check confirms the role is genuine, the employment terms meet INZ requirements (including pay at or above the median wage for most roles), and that you have made genuine attempts to recruit NZ workers. NZIC can prepare the Job Check documentation and advise on advertising requirements.</div></div>
      </div>
      <div class="faq-item">
        <button class="faq-question" aria-expanded="false">We found a great candidate but are not yet accredited — what should we do? <span class="faq-chevron" aria-hidden="true">▾</span></button>
        <div class="faq-answer"><div class="faq-answer-inner">Start the accreditation process immediately. The candidate can be held on a visitor visa or extend their current visa while accreditation is in progress. NZIC can coordinate both the employer's accreditation and the worker's visa status simultaneously.</div></div>
      </div>
