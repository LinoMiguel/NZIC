      <h2>Why job hunting from overseas is difficult</h2>
      <p>New Zealand employers overwhelmingly prefer candidates they can meet, who already have NZ work rights, and who understand local workplace culture. From overseas, you have none of those advantages — and you are competing against local applicants for the small number of roles that are publicly advertised.</p>

      <p>Most overseas job seekers make the same mistake: they apply to advertised roles online and wait. Because 70–75% of NZ roles are filled through networks and direct introductions (the "hidden job market"), that approach misses the majority of opportunities.</p>

      <h2>How it relates to your visa</h2>
      <p>Many skilled migrants need a job offer to trigger their visa pathway (Skilled Migrant Category, AEWV). The job search and visa processes are therefore interdependent. NZIC's <a href="/about/fusion-service">Fusion Service</a> manages them together — the same team, coordinated timelines.</p>

      <h2>AI and the NZ job market</h2>
      <p>AI tools are increasingly being used for job searching. NZIC's consultants help you use AI tools effectively while keeping the human element that matters most in the NZ job market: personal connections, cultural fluency, and credibility.</p>
