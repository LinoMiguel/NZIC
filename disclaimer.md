      <p>The content on this website is provided for general information purposes only. It does not constitute immigration advice under the Immigration Advisers Licensing Act 2007. Reading or relying on information from this website does not create an adviser-client relationship between you and New Zealand Immigration Concepts Ltd.</p>

      <p>Immigration law and INZ policy change frequently. While we make every effort to keep the information on this site accurate and current, we cannot guarantee that every page reflects the most up-to-date requirements at any given time. Always verify critical figures — points thresholds, financial amounts, processing times — against the official INZ source, which is linked from each relevant page.</p>

      <h2>When NZIC acts for you</h2>
