      <h2>Active Investor Plus Resident Visa</h2>
      <p>The Active Investor Plus visa replaced the previous Investor and Investor Plus categories. It has two investment tiers:</p>

      <table>
        <thead><tr><th>Category</th><th>Minimum investment</th><th>Investment type</th><th>Residency requirement</th></tr></thead>
        <tbody>
          <tr><td><strong>Growth</strong></td><td>NZD 5 million</td><td>Direct investment in NZ businesses (not listed equities, property, or bonds)</td><td>117 days over 4 years</td></tr>
          <tr><td><strong>Balanced</strong></td><td>NZD 15 million</td><td>Wider range: includes listed equities, managed funds, and philanthropic donations</td><td>117 days over 4 years</td></tr>
        </tbody>
      </table>

      <p>Both categories require:</p>
      <ul>
        <li>Investment committed for a minimum of 4 years</li>
        <li>Evidence that the funds are sourced legitimately</li>
        <li>Compliance with NZ securities law and anti-money-laundering requirements</li>
        <li>Meeting INZ's health and character requirements</li>
      </ul>

      <h2>Business Investor Resident Visa</h2>
      <p>The Business Investor pathway suits entrepreneurs and business owners who want to establish or purchase a business in New Zealand. It requires:</p>
      <ul>
        <li>At least NZD 3 million in business investment</li>
        <li>Significant business experience (typically 3+ years owning or directing a business)</li>
        <li>A credible business plan approved by INZ</li>
        <li>An initial 3-year work visa, transitioning to residence on meeting investment and business requirements</li>
      </ul>

      <h2>Acceptable investments</h2>
      <p>INZ has detailed rules on what qualifies as acceptable investment for each category. Residential property, most government securities, and cash deposits generally do not qualify for the Growth category. Your adviser will confirm what applies to your situation.</p>
