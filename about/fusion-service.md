      <h2>The Catch-22 explained</h2>
      <p>New Zealand's immigration system creates a frustrating dilemma for many skilled migrants:</p>
      <blockquote>
        To get a visa, you often need a job offer. But to get a job offer, NZ employers often want someone who already has work rights. And to get work rights, you need a job offer.
      </blockquote>
      <p>This circular problem stops many qualified people from even starting their application. NZIC's Fusion Service is built to break that cycle.</p>

      <h2>What the Fusion Service is</h2>
      <p>The Fusion Service pairs a <strong>Licensed Immigration Adviser</strong> with a <strong>Job Search Consultant</strong> to work on your situation simultaneously. The two streams are coordinated — when the job comes, the visa team is ready to move immediately, and the visa strategy is built around realistic job timelines.</p>

      <h2>Why it works</h2>
      <p>The two streams talk to each other. If your visa pathway requires a job offer within a specific timeframe, the job search consultant knows that and works accordingly. If a job offer arrives sooner than expected, the visa adviser can accelerate the application. This coordination is what separates the Fusion Service from going to two separate firms.</p>

      <h2>Who it is for</h2>
      <p>The Fusion Service suits skilled migrants who:</p>
      <ul>
        <li>Have qualifications and experience that meet INZ thresholds but need a job offer to trigger their visa</li>
        <li>Are considering the Skilled Migrant Category, Accredited Employer Work Visa, or other employer-linked pathways</li>
        <li>Want to move to New Zealand rather than simply obtain a visa — meaning the job search is as important as the application</li>
        <li>Have tried job hunting from overseas and found it difficult without local support and contacts</li>
      </ul>

      <h2>Getting started</h2>
      <p>The first step is always the same: submit a free assessment. A Licensed Immigration Adviser reviews your situation and advises whether the Fusion Service is the right fit or whether a visa-only or job-search-only approach makes more sense.</p>
